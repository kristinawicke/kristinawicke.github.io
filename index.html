About

My research lies at the intersection of mathematical phylogenetics, evolutionary biology, and discrete mathematics. I am interested in developing mathematical and computational methods for understanding evolutionary relationships and analyzing complex biological data.

I am particularly interested in the mathematical structures underlying phylogenetic trees and networks, as well as the algorithms and computational complexity questions that arise from them.
